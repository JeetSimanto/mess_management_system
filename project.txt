================================================================================
MESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - FEATURE SPECIFICATION & DOCUMENTATION
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
The Mess Management System Template is a comprehensive, structured financial 
and operational tracking android app designed for shared housing, hostels, 
bachelor mess setups, and communal living groups. It automates expense tracking,
daily meal counting, member fund contributions, meal rate calculations, equal 
utility sharing, and monthly individual financial settlements.


1. GROCERY PURCHASES TRACKING (GROCERY)
-------------------------------------------------
Key Purpose:
  - Records all food, raw ingredients, and daily grocery expenses incurred for 
    the mess.

Core Features & Structure:
  - Line-by-Line Entry: Captures Date, Item Name, Unit of Measurement (e.g., kg, 
    pcs, liters), Quantity, Total Cost in BDT, and 
    descriptive Notes (optional).
  - Automated Grocery Total: Automatically calculates the aggregate monthly food 
    expenditure (TOTAL GROCERY EXPENSES).

2. UTILITY & HOUSEHOLD EXPENSES MANAGEMENT (UTILITY)
----------------------------------------------------------
Key Purpose:
  - Tracks shared living expenses independent of meal costs, ensuring non-food 
    bills are categorized and divided fairly.

Core Features & Structure:
  - Bill Categorization: Tracks bill types such as House Rent (basa vara), Water 
    Bill (pani), Waste Collection (moila), Transport/Market Travel (bazar jatayat), 
    and Utensil Supplies (ghor jharu, ghor muchni, knife, basket).
  - Notes.
  - Automated Utility Total: Aggregates total monthly utility and maintenance costs 
    (TOTAL UTILITY).


3. DAILY MEAL TRACKER (DAILY MEAL TRACKING)
--------------------------------------------
Key Purpose:
  - Tracks daily meal consumption per member to ensure meal costs are allocated 
    proportionally based on actual consumption.

Core Features & Structure:
  - Monthly Calendar Grid: Columns for Days 1 through 31, with individual columns 
    for each member (e.g., Jeet, Sagnik, Antu, Noyon).
  - Daily Total Counter: Automatically sums meals consumed across all members on a 
    given day (DAILY TOTAL).
  - Individual Monthly Totals: Sums total meals eaten by each member at the end of 
    the month, as well as the collective mess meal grand total (TOTAL MEALS CONSUMED).


4. MEMBER CONTRIBUTIONS & DEPOSIT LOG (MEMBER CONTRIBUTIONS)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Key Purpose:
  - Registers all monetary deposits, advances, and bill payments made by mess 
    members throughout the month.

Core Features & Structure:
  - Detailed Deposit Ledger: Records Member Name, Contribution Amount (BDT), 
    Date Paid, and Purpose Note 
  - Member-Wise Aggregate Table: Automatically sums total contributions per person 
    (TOTAL CONTRIBUTIONS PER MEMBER).
  - Grand Total Fund: Displays the cumulative fund collected across all members 
    (GRAND TOTAL CONTRIBUTIONS).


5. DASHBOARD(homepage)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Purpose:
  - Performs all backend mathematical logic to determine individual cost 
    responsibilities, meal rates, and final balance settlements.

Sub-Features & Analytical Components:

  A. Expense Overview
     - Summarizes Total Grocery Expenses, Total Utility Expenses, Combined Total 
       Mess Expenses, Total Member Contributions, and Overall Surplus/Deficit.

  B. Dynamic Meal Rate Calculation
     - Formula: Meal Rate = Total Grocery Expenses / Total Meals Consumed.
     - Ensures meal costs cover only raw food expenses, keeping food expenses 
       strictly variable per meal eaten.

  C. Equal Utility Division
     - Formula: Utility Share per Member = Total Utility Expenses / Number of Members.
     - Fixed head-count division for common house costs (rent, water, utilities).

  D. Member-Wise Cost Breakdown Table
     - Calculates individual Grocery Cost: (Individual Meals × Calculated Meal Rate).
     - Adds fixed Utility Share per member.
     - Derives Total Individual Cost: (Individual Grocery Cost + Individual Utility Share).
     - Compares Total Individual Cost against Total Individual Contribution.
     - Computes Net Individual Balance: (Contribution - Total Individual Cost).

  E. Final Settlement Status & Action Required
     - Provides clear, actionable financial directives for every member:
       * Positive Balance (+): Displays "GET BACK BDT X" (Member paid more than 
         their fair share and is due a refund).
       * Negative Balance (-): Displays "PAY EXTRA BDT Y" (Member underpaid and 
         must deposit additional funds to clear their balance).


6. WORKFLOW & SYSTEM USAGE HIGHLIGHTS
------------------------------------
  - Transparency: Distinguishes variable meal costs from fixed house utilities, 
    preventing non-diners from paying for food costs while maintaining equal rent/utility splitting.
  - Payment Transparency: Supports multiple payment methods (Bkash, Cash) with 
    traceable date stamps and notes.
  - End-of-Month Automated Reconciliation: Removes manual math errors and provides 
    instant refund or pay-up instructions for final account closure.
